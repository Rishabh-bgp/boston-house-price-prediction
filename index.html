Fair Housing Note

The original data contain a column B derived from the share of Black residents in each town. It encodes race, so it was removed from this model. LSTAT (share of "lower status" residents) is kept as a neighbourhood socioeconomic measure, with that caveat. Values are capped at $50k in the source data and reflect 1970, not today.
